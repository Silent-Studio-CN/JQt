## 中文版

SilentStudio 为需要**豁免开源义务**的商业用户提供商业许可。

### 价格模型

| 项目 | 条款 |
|------|------|
| 触发条件 | 用户累计营收 ≥ $1,000,000（见 LICENSE.md 第 3.3 条） |
| 费用 | 用户基于 JQt 的产品/服务**年利润的 5%**，按年支付 |
| 权益 | 豁免开源义务（LICENSE.md 第 3.2(b) 条） |
| 保留义务 | 署名义务（LICENSE.md 第 3.1 条）始终有效 |
| 提前购买 | 累计营收未达阈值时也可随时提前签署 |

### 如何获取

- 联系 SilentStudio（联系方式待定，可先通过仓库 Issue 沟通意向）
- 正式授权以双方签署的**书面商业许可协议**为准，本文件仅为要约概述
- 商业许可仅覆盖 JQt 自身代码；Qt 运行时的义务（LGPLv3）不受影响，见 THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md

## English Version

SilentStudio offers a commercial license to business users who need to be **exempted from the Open Source Obligation**.

### Pricing Model

| Item | Terms |
|------|-------|
| Trigger condition | User's Cumulative Revenue ≥ $1,000,000 (see LICENSE.md Section 3.3) |
| Fee | **5% of the annual profit** derived from the User's products/services based on JQt, paid annually |
| Rights granted | Exemption from the Open Source Obligation (LICENSE.md Section 3.2(b)) |
| Retained obligation | Attribution obligation (LICENSE.md Section 3.1) remains in full force |
| Early purchase | May be executed at any time before the Cumulative Revenue threshold is reached |

### How to Obtain

- Contact SilentStudio (contact channel TBD; intent may be communicated via repository Issues)
- Formal authorization is governed by the **written commercial license agreement** executed by both parties; this document is only a summary of the offer
- The commercial license covers JQt's own code only; obligations for the Qt runtime (LGPLv3) are unaffected — see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md
